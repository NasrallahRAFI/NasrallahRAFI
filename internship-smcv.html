Multidisciplinary Project

Automation (PLC/HMI), Mechanical Arm and Pneumatic Press Design, and IT Maintenance

**Company:** SMCV (Société Marocaine des Compteurs) | **Duration:** July 2025 – September 2025

#PLC #MechanicalDesign #ITMaintenance

Core Objectives

This project aimed to consolidate skills in automation, mechatronic design, and industrial IT, while providing concrete solutions to operational issues encountered at the SMCV workshop.

  • **Automation and Supervision:** Development of PLC and HMI programs for the automation and supervision of industrial machines, specifically plastic injection machines and insertion presses.

  • **Mechatronic Design:** Conception and 3D modeling of mechatronic devices, such as a nut insertion arm and a custom-designed pneumatic press.

  • **IT Maintenance:** Performing IT maintenance, including operating system repair and configuration, as well as installing specialized industrial software.
